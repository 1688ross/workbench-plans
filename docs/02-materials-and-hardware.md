## Consumables and shop supplies

Sandpaper 80/120/180, blue tape, painter's plastic, clamps (you have those), a 3/4" brad-point or Forstner bit and a 3/4" dog-hole jig (or a drill guide), 1/2" and 1/4" router bits for the bit boards, a 3/4" straight bit and a 3/8" straight bit for the T-track dados, a 1/2" spiral for the plate recess, a 45-deg chamfer bit for the finger pulls.
