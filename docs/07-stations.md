# How each station works

## Outfeed (north)

Roll the saw against the bench so its rear-rail face touches the bridge lip. The top sits 1/16" below the saw table so stock never catches; the two grooves take a miter gauge bar or a sled runner for 14". Anything ripped travels the full 96" over a flat top because the miter saw is below it. The motor bay behind the north face gives the CNS motor 15" of room and hides the saw's 4" hose.

## Hand-work and clamping station (west)

- **Vise:** 9" quick-release at the NW corner, maple-lined, dog in the front jaw in line with the first column of holes.
- **Dog field:** 81 holes, 4" grid, 2-1/4" of material. Rockler dogs, holdfasts, hold-downs and surface clamps all work.
- **Clamping station:** drop the six pipe-clamp cradles into the holes at the spacing you need and lay the pipe clamps in them; panels glue up flat and square. Cauls clamp to the dog holes. The 1" overhang on the west and south edges takes F-clamps anywhere.
- **Downdraft:** open the plenum gate (service-gap side, reach through the band-panel clean-out) and the whole field pulls sanding dust down. Close it when clamping so you are not fighting the vacuum.
- **Drawers:** W1 has three drawers (top one is the switched charging drawer); W2 has the laser tray over a deep drawer.

## Router station (east)

- Raise the bit with the Rout-R-Lift crank through the plate; the plate is flush when the bit is retracted, so the top stays a bench.
- Clamp your fence into the two T-tracks 26" apart; slide it to the bit. Plug the fence hose into the flip-lid port in the edge band.
- Featherboards and a miter gauge use the combo track 4-1/2" from the edge.
- Bits live in the vertical pull-out beside the doors: pull it out, take the bit, push it back. Collets and wrenches on its top shelf.
- The router box is sealed and ducted; open its gate on the spine (inside the box floor) before routing.

## Miter station (south)

1. Lift the two hatch leaves out (finger holes at the outer ends) and slide them into the slot beside the lift bay; lift out the centre bar and drop it in with them.
2. Pump the foot pedal until the sub-platform meets the stops. The saw's deck is now flush with the top and the hood is behind the blade.
3. Raise the drop-leaves on both ends; the folding brackets lock. You now have 46" of support each side and 96" of flat top to the north.
4. Open the miter hood gate (4") and the chute gate (2-1/2"); switch the saw on with the paddle on the front panel.
5. Done: switch off, close gates, open the release valve on the lift (slow), the saw sinks below the top; refit the bar and leaves; drop the wings.

Long stock: the north half of the bench takes anything up to 8' to the left or right when you angle it across the top; for 12' stock use a roller stand beyond a drop-leaf.

## Laser well (top, north-west)

- Flat work: leave the insert in; the laser sits on the top like any desk.
- Rotary work: lift the insert out (finger hole), drop the floor panel onto the cleats at the depth you need (2", 4-1/2" or 7"), set the laser over the opening so the rotary attachment hangs inside. Plug into the W2 outlet; run the exhaust hose to a window.
- Storage: pull the tray out of W2, set the laser on it, push it home.
